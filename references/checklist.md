# Scientific English revision checklist

## Reader sequence

The paragraph should help the reader answer, in order:

1. Why is this relevant?
2. What is new or unresolved?
3. Why should the reader trust the claim?
4. Can the result or method be reused?
5. What is the boundary of the conclusion?

## Paragraph functions

- Context/background
- Prior knowledge
- Knowledge gap
- Research objective/question
- Methods/approach
- Results/observation
- Comparison
- Mechanistic interpretation
- Implication
- Limitation
- Transition

## Core organization patterns

- Classification
- Definition
- Process
- Cause–effect
- Generalization followed by specific development
- Situation–Problem–Solution–Evaluation
- Problem–Solution–Evidence

## Common revision targets

- Ambiguous `which` clauses
- Unsupported causal verbs
- Overstated novelty or universality
- Weak verb + abstract noun
- Excessive passive voice
- Excessive nominalization
- Unnecessary relative clauses
- Repetition and empty phrases
- Unclear pronoun reference
- Inconsistent terminology or abbreviations
- Unreported comparison, baseline, sample size, uncertainty, or statistical evidence
